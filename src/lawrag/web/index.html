Scope, Methodology & Legal Notice

Verification Rules (D1–D7)

  • D1 / D2: Cited UIDs exist in the graph and were supplied in context.
  • D3: Numeric punishments, imprisonment terms, and fines match source text.
  • D4 / D5: IPC↔BNS relationship claims match graph mappings with source evidence.
  • D6: Temporal cutoff honors 2024-06-30 (IPC) vs 2024-07-01 (BNS).
  • D7: False premises (e.g., repealed/nonexistent section queries) are surfaced clearly.

Corpus Coverage & Limits

  • Substantive offences under IPC (1860) and BNS (2023) only.
  • Procedure (CrPC / BNSS), Evidence (IEA / BSA), and case-law outcomes are out of scope.
  • “No direct equivalent” is stated only when a recorded OMITTED / NEW mapping exists; unmapped sections mean unreviewed.
  • Not legal advice. Concordance rows marked SEED-DRAFT await formal advocate review.
